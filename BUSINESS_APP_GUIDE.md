# Universal Business Management App Guide

## 🎯 Overview

This is a **complete business management system** for ANY type of business - retail, wholesale, manufacturing, services, and more. It's not limited to ply/hardware businesses!

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. **Dashboard (Home)**
- Sales & Receipt summaries
- Quick action cards for all operations
- Daily summary at a glance
- Recent activity tracking

### 2. **Vouchers & Documents**
**Create:**
- 📝 Invoice
- 🛒 Purchase
- ↩️ Sales Return  
- 🛍️ Order
- 💰 Receipt
- 📋 Quotation
- 💳 Payment
- 👥 Party Management

**View & Share:**
- 📄 Invoices
- 📚 Account Ledger
- 🛍️ Orders
- 💵 Bills Receivable/Payable

### 3. **Reports**
**Accounts Book:**
- Day Book
- Daily Summary
- Account Ledger
- Sales Reports
- Payment/Receipt Tracking
- Cash & Bank Reports
- Trial Balance

**Outstanding Analysis:**
- Bills Receivable/Payable
- Amount Receivable
- Ageing Analysis (Receivables/Payables)

**Inventory:**
- Stock Status
- Group Stock Status
- Low Stock Alerts
- Out of Stock Alerts

### 4. **Parties (Customers & Suppliers)**
- Sundry Debtors (Customers)
- Sundry Creditors (Suppliers)
- Overdue tracking
- Balance management
- Location-based organization

### 5. **Inventory Management**
- Universal product categories (not just ply/hardware)
- Dynamic fields based on product type
- Stock tracking & history
- Low stock alerts
- Barcode/SKU support
- Multi-unit support (pieces, kg, liters, etc.)

### 6. **Settings (More)**
- Company/Business management
- Multi-company support
- Smart alerts & notifications
- Approval workflows
- Financial year settings

- User management

## 🚀 Getting Started

### For Store Owners
1. **Register Your Business**
   - Enter business name, type, contact details
   - Set up GST number (if applicable)
   - Create admin account

2. **Add Products/Services**
   - Navigate to Inventory
   - Add items with details
   - Set minimum stock levels

3. **Add Parties**
   - Create customer/supplier records
   - Track balances and dues

4. **Start Creating Vouchers**
   - Create invoices for sales
   - Record purchases
   - Track payments & receipts

5. **Monitor Reports**
   - Check daily summaries
   - View stock status
   - Track outstanding amounts

### For Employees
- Limited access based on role
- Can create vouchers
- View assigned reports
- Cannot access financial settings

## 💡 Key Differences from Generic Inventory Apps

✅ **Complete Accounting** - Not just inventory, includes full accounting module
✅ **Voucher System** - Professional invoicing, receipts, payments
✅ **Party Management** - Track customers & suppliers separately
✅ **Outstanding Management** - Aging analysis, receivables, payables
✅ **Multi-Company** - Manage multiple businesses in one app
✅ **Approval Workflows** - Control over voucher approvals
✅ **Smart Alerts** - Notifications for receivables, low stock, etc.
